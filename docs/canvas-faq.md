# Penn State Canvas FAQ

## Getting Started & Access

**Q: How do I log in to Penn State Canvas?**
A: Go to psu.instructure.com and sign in using your Penn State Access Account credentials (xyz123@psu.edu) along with multifactor authentication (MFA). Make sure your MFA devices and browser settings are up to date before classes begin.

**Q: When do my courses become available on the first day of classes?**
A: Course spaces generally open after 10:00 a.m. (ET) on the first day of the semester, provided your instructor has published the course.

**Q: Is there an official tutorial to learn how Canvas works at Penn State?**
A: Yes. You can take the self-paced Student Orientation course hosted directly inside Canvas to practice navigating courses, submitting assignments, and configuring your account settings before the semester starts.

## Enrollment, Rosters & Missing Courses

**Q: Why isn't one of my registered classes showing up on my Canvas Dashboard?**
A: There are three common reasons:

1. Unpublished Course. Your instructor has not yet published the course space. Click Courses > All Courses in the left navigation bar to check if the course is listed under Future Enrollments as unpublished.
2. LionPATH Sync Delay. Canvas automatically syncs class rosters with LionPATH. If you recently added or swapped a class, allow time for LionPATH to update Canvas before contacting support.
3. Enrollment Hold or Error. Verify in LionPATH that your registration went through cleanly. If it has been over 24 hours since enrolling and the semester has started, email ITservicedesk@psu.edu or reach out to your instructor.

**Q: Where did my courses from previous semesters go?**
A: To comply with Penn State’s General Retention Schedule for student grades and keep the system running smoothly, Penn State IT periodically archives older courses. Once archived, a course no longer appears on your Dashboard or in your All Courses list. If you want to keep personal copies of syllabi, rubrics, or past submissions, download them before the scheduled archive date.

## Daily Coursework, Communication & Notifications

**Q: How do I communicate with my instructors and classmates in Canvas?**
A: Use the Inbox tool on the far-left global navigation bar to send direct internal messages to instructors and peers. Inside individual courses, you can also interact via Announcements, Discussion Boards, and Groups for team projects. (Note: Keep checking your official Penn State Outlook email as well, as some instructors send messages outside Canvas.)

**Q: How do I avoid missing assignment deadlines?**
A: Customize your alerts under Account > Notifications to receive email, text, or push notifications for due dates, announcements, and grade postings. You can also view aggregated deadlines across all your classes in the global Calendar tab or use the Canvas Student mobile app.

**Q: How is "attendance" tracked in online Canvas courses?**
A: Simply logging into Canvas does not count as attendance. Penn State defines online attendance as documentable, active participation—such as interacting with your instructor or classmates, participating in discussions, or submitting timed assignments.

## Proctoring, Tools & Technical Support

**Q: Do I need special software for proctored exams or third-party tools?**
A: Some courses use integrated tools (such as Honorlock, Turnitin, Kaltura, or publisher courseware) via single sign-on inside Canvas. Check your course syllabus during the first week to see if exam proctoring or specific browser extensions are required and schedule proctored exams early.

**Q: Who do I contact if something in Canvas isn't working?**
A:

- 24/7 Canvas Support. Click the Help icon at the bottom of the left-hand navigation menu inside Canvas for 24/7 live chat or phone support.
- Penn State IT Service Desk. For Penn State login, MFA, or LionPATH-to-Canvas enrollment sync issues, email ITservicedesk@psu.edu or call 814-865-HELP (4357).
